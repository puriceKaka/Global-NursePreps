Cookie Policy

Global NursePrep may use cookies and local browser storage to keep the platform reliable, secure, and easier for students to use.

Cookie structure used on the site

  • Essential cookies: login sessions, security checks, course access, payment status, and exam continuity.
  • Preference cookies: language choice, light or dark mode, saved navigation state, and accessibility preferences.
  • Learning cookies: course progress, unfinished lessons, quiz attempts, and dashboard shortcuts.
  • Analytics cookies: anonymous page performance, feature usage, and error reports to improve the platform.
  • Support cookies: puriceAI, contact form context, and support request continuity.

Students can clear cookies in their browser, but some protected learning, exam, and payment features may stop working until they sign in again.
