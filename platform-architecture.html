Scalable Platform Architecture

Global NursePrep should be designed as a distributed, highly available learning platform that can handle traffic growth and continue running even when one component fails.

Scalability and availability structure

  • Stateless application services behind a load balancer so traffic can be shared across multiple servers.
  • Managed database with backups, read replicas, monitoring, and point-in-time recovery.
  • Separate services for authentication, courses, payments, exams, files, notifications, and analytics.
  • Object storage and CDN delivery for videos, PDFs, certificates, and downloadable learning materials.
  • Queue-based background jobs for emails, notifications, certificate generation, reports, and video processing.
  • Health checks, auto-restart, horizontal scaling, and failover so healthy components keep serving students if another component fails.
  • Multi-zone deployment, logging, alerting, rate limiting, and security controls for stable traffic management.

The goal is a resilient platform where course learning, payments, exams, and support remain available through distributed services and controlled failure isolation.
