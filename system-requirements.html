System Requirements

Global NursePrep works on modern phones, tablets, laptops, and desktops with a stable internet connection for video classes, exams, payments, and downloads.

Recommended setup

  • Latest Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari browser.
  • Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, or Linux device.
  • At least 2 GB RAM for smooth video learning and mock exams.
  • Stable internet connection for live classes and timed exams.
  • PDF reader and enabled browser storage for notes and progress.
