Privacy Policy

Global NursePrep protects learner information and supports Kenya data protection rights. This page explains the type of information used to run learning, payment, support, and exam services.

Data we handle

  • Account details such as name, email, phone number, and student role.
  • Learning records including course progress, quiz scores, certificates, and exam analytics.
  • Payment confirmation details needed to unlock purchased courses only.
  • Support messages sent through contact forms, Purice AI, or consultation requests.

Student data should only be used for platform services, academic support, compliance, security, and communication with the learner.
