Accessibility Assistance

Global NursePrep aims to support learners using mobile devices, keyboard navigation, readable text, clear contrast, and accessible learning materials.

Help available

  • Support for account access, course navigation, and downloadable notes.
  • Help with readable formats for notes, assignments, and revision material.
  • Guidance for learners who need extra support during online learning or exams.
  • Contact support through WhatsApp or the contact page for accessibility requests.
