Global NursePrep Cares

Global NursePrep Cares is the platform's student support and access initiative for learners who need guidance, mentorship, and practical help while preparing for nursing exams.

Support focus

  • Study planning and revision guidance for busy healthcare learners.
  • Academic support for proposals, reports, presentations, and dissemination preparation.
  • Accessibility assistance and Purice AI support for learners who need help using the platform.
